# Tanuhomes quick start

Boogbe keeps every stay in one place: bookings, payments and your Airbnb/Booking.com calendars. Open **https://app.boogbe.com** on a phone or a computer.

## 1. Sign in
1. Go to https://app.boogbe.com and sign in with your email and password.
2. Pick **Tanuhomes** on "Choose an operator". If you belong to more than one business, you can switch later from the top-left corner.
3. You land on the **Calendar**: one row per unit, with stays shown as bars.

Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page and a reset link is emailed to you.

## 2. Invite your staff
1. Open **Team** in the menu.
2. Enter their email, choose a role, and press **Invite**.
   - **Admin:** everything, including settings and money corrections.
   - **Front desk:** bookings, guests and payments.
   - **Housekeeper:** cleaning tasks only.
   - **Landlord:** a property owner's view of their own units.
3. They get an email with a link. They set their name and password, and they're in. You can cancel a pending invitation from the same page.

## 3. Add a booking
1. On the **Calendar**, tap an empty day on the unit's row. Or open **Bookings** and press **New booking**.
2. Pick the guest, or press **New guest** and add their name and phone number.
3. Check the dates and the number of guests. The price is worked out from the unit's nightly rate; add a reason if you change it.
4. Press **Save booking**. If the dates overlap another stay, Boogbe refuses and tells you which one.

A booking starts **Tentative** (dates held). It becomes **Confirmed** when you confirm it or record the first payment.

## 4. Record a payment
1. Open the booking (tap its bar on the Calendar, or find it in **Bookings**).
2. In **Payments**, press **Record payment**. Enter the amount, method (bank transfer, cash, POS, card) and the transfer reference, then **Save**.
3. The balance updates straight away, and the receipt number becomes a link that downloads a PDF receipt for the guest.
4. Caution deposits go under **Deposit**: record what you received, and later what you returned or kept, with a reason. Refunds go under **Refund**.
5. Made a mistake? Ask an admin to **Void** the entry. It stays in the history, struck through. Money records are never edited or deleted.

**Money** in the menu shows what came in this month by method, refunds, deposits held, and who still owes.

## 5. Connect an Airbnb calendar
Do this for each unit listed on Airbnb (the same steps work for Booking.com and VRBO).
1. Open **Properties**, choose the property, and press **Calendar sync** on the unit.
2. **Bring bookings in:**
   - In Airbnb, open the listing → Availability → Calendar sync, and copy the **export** link.
   - In Boogbe, choose the channel **Airbnb**, give it a name, paste the link into **Calendar link**, and save.
   - Airbnb's dates show on your Calendar as blocked within about 15 minutes, and again every 15 minutes after that.
3. **Send availability out:**
   - Copy Boogbe's **Airbnb** link from this section.
   - In Airbnb, use **Import calendar** and paste it. Airbnb then sees the stays you take on WhatsApp or by phone. Airbnb refreshes on its own schedule, sometimes a few hours.
   - Each channel gets its own link, so Airbnb never sees its own bookings echoed back.
4. If a channel's dates ever overlap a booking you took, Boogbe flags it on the Calendar and under **Conflicts**, and emails admins. Open it and choose what happened:
   - handled on the channel;
   - cancel our booking, with a refund;
   - moved our booking.

Keep the Boogbe calendar links private: anyone with a link can see that unit's busy dates (never guest details).
